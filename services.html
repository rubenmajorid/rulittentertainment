LED Uplighting

Uplighting

Transform your venue with professional LED uplighting placed throughout the room to add color, depth and atmosphere.

Uplighting can be customized to match your wedding colors, event theme or venue décor, and can completely change the look of a space once the lights go down.

Perfect for

  • Quinceañeras
  • Birthdays

  • Private parties

Dancing on the Clouds

Create a memorable first-dance moment with our Dancing on the Clouds effect.

A low-lying cloud effect covers the dance floor, creating the appearance that you're dancing above the clouds while keeping the effect close to the floor for photos and video.

Perfect for

  • Bride & Groom First Dance
  • Quinceañera Waltz
  • Sweet 16s
  • Grand Entrances
  • Special Spotlight Moments

Available with select packages. Pairs especially well with cold sparks, uplighting and professional dance-floor lighting.

CO₂ Club Cannon

Bring high-energy nightclub effects directly to your event with our CO₂ Club Cannon.
